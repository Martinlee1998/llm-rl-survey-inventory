public_sources.csv / public_sources.json
    One record for each of the 52 sources cited in the current manuscript:
    37 primary method or empirical papers, fourteen related surveys, and one
    foundational theory paper. Each record identifies the bibliographic work,
    the version actually consulted, public access links, subject domain, role,
    and current manuscript sections.

evidence_matrix.csv / evidence_matrix.json
    82 source-linked records connecting particular claims to original-paper
    pages, figures, tables, equations, protocols, and interpretive limits.
    A source may support more than one record. These are evidence records,
    not 82 independent experiments.

math/
    The extended analytical note, its source, references, reproduction code,
    and numerical results. See math/README.txt for instructions and scope.

The CSV and JSON files contain the same records and field values. CSV files
use UTF-8 with a byte-order mark for spreadsheet compatibility; JSON files
use UTF-8 and contain an array of objects. They can be read without software
specific to the authoring environment. Public links replace local file paths.

HOW TO READ THE SOURCE REGISTER

citation_key links a source to the manuscript bibliography and evidence table.
bibliographic_identity describes its publication; read_version and read_url
identify the text consulted. A formally published work may have been read in
an identified author manuscript. This distinction is explicit, including for
Cao's survey. additional_read_urls is populated only when another version
supplies evidence used here. For Robometer, formal RSS pages and extended
arXiv pages are distinguished. acceptance_evidence_url supplies the official
NeurIPS 2026 evidence for SOLE-R1, whose technical text was read as arXiv v2. ManipGen uses its identified
ICRA 2025 accepted author version, as stated on the official arXiv record.

current_sections records section labels or source-file stems from the current
manuscript. These identify locations, not local filesystem paths.

HOW TO READ THE EVIDENCE MATRIX

manuscript_location identifies the section and subsection; manuscript_anchor
is a short phrase locating the relevant passage. current_claim states the
specific point supported. source_locator and pdf_pages identify its basis.

comparison, conditions_held, intervention_or_mechanism, source_observation,
and training_budget_and_repetition record the comparison and its protocol.
interpretive_boundary states the limit that matters for the inference. An
empty field means it is not applicable or not established in this record;
it does not mean that a condition was experimentally controlled.

Pages are 1-based physical PDF pages unless a record explicitly says otherwise.
Special cases:
- LIMEN: the RLJ cover is PDF page 1; the main paper starts on PDF page 2.
- ExploRLLM: two institutional cover pages precede the paper; PDF page 3 is
  paper page 1 and printed page 9011.
- LgTS: PDF page 1 is printed page 1736.
- CALM: PDF page 1 is printed page 8736.
- Robometer: the record names the formal RSS or extended arXiv version.

Source observations and review-authored analysis have different roles.
Records E056-E058 identify source formulas used by the analytical comparisons;
the constructions themselves belong to this review, not to the cited papers.
Related-survey records E059-E067 and E072 establish prior coverage and attribution.
They are not additional primary experiments supporting a method comparison.
E068-E069 document existing normalization analyses and their remedies;
E070 attributes the planning/locality mechanism to ManipGen, while E071
records its separate initialization-region ablation. E073 records PSL's
negative control adding wrist inputs to global-view baseline observations.
E074 records EXIMO's separate offline-to-online countercase with unguided
base inputs and action-preserving residual relabeling. E075 and E077-E079 record EnvGen controls and the Craftax relation,
allocation, and objective-weight comparisons. E080-E081 attribute earlier
environment and curriculum syntheses. E082 records MaestroMotif's episode-
organization comparison, and E083 records WebRL's task/replay controls.
E085-E086 attribute prior
on-policy-distillation synthesis and local gradient-alignment analysis.
E087-E088 record SOAR's target-improvement feedback and its equal-size
teacher-sampling comparison with fresh students on held-out questions.
E089 attributes related analysis of data exposure, adaptation, and component
evaluation to the October 2026 Physical AI survey.
These records do not turn cross-paper comparisons into shared experiments.
Evidence IDs are stable identifiers, so their numbering is not contiguous.
E024, E034, E035, E047, E076, and E084 were retired when their manuscript contrasts were removed;
E046 was retired in an earlier revision. Their absence does not mean missing data.

The register documents the evidence used in this manuscript. It is not an
exhaustive field bibliography, a pooled effect-size dataset, or a count of
independent replications. Within-paper controls are recorded separately from
the review's cross-paper interpretation. Training budgets, denominators,
repetition counts, and the object transferred remain specific to each study.
